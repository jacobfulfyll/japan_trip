Culture and Practical Prep

Fast etiquette and phrase checklist for on-the-ground confidence.

Etiquette Essentials

  • Quiet voices on transit.
  • Queue clearly for trains and escalators.
  • No tipping in normal service contexts.
  • Remove shoes where required.
  • Carry a small trash bag.
  • Shrine flow: bow, cleanse, offer, bow-clap-clap-bow.

Dining Basics

  • Use oshibori before meal.
  • Say itadakimasu before eating.
  • Say gochisosama deshita after eating.
  • Use serving chopsticks for shared dishes.
  • Avoid chopstick-to-chopstick food passing.

Useful Phrases

  • Sumimasen - Excuse me / sorry.
  • Arigatou gozaimasu - Thank you.
  • Onegaishimasu - Please.
  • Kore o kudasai - This one please.
  • Osusume wa? - What do you recommend?
  • Eigo menu wa arimasu ka? - English menu?
  • Daijoubu desu - I am okay / no thanks.
  • Toire wa doko desu ka? - Where is restroom?
  • Eki wa doko desu ka? - Where is station?
  • Kampai - Cheers.
